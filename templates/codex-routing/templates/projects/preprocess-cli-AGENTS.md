# preprocess-cli project boundary

Treat this repository as a production preprocessing boundary. Focus review and implementation on multi-segment and two-segment coordinate transforms, ICP, pose graphs, fusion configuration, input manifests, atomic publication, recovery, and production preprocessing contracts.

Coordinate mathematics, coordinate direction, pose propagation, approval or transaction design, and final acceptance must be decided or reviewed by the primary agent. Subagents may gather evidence or implement a narrowly specified change, but they must stop and escalate when the contract or safety boundary is unclear.

Preserve input and output contracts, keep publication recoverable, and report exact validation commands and evidence. Keep project configuration free of local paths, datasets, and business data.
