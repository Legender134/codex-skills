# Evidence ledger (draft)

## Authorization

Record whether identity uncertainty, installation, integration, or
publication has explicit approval. Creation alone does not grant any of
these authorities.

## Source roles

For each source, record its identifier, provenance, allowed role or roles,
scope, and any prohibited use. A source governs only the roles supported by
the recorded evidence.

## Next gate

Name the next applicable identity, action, format, visual, runtime, or
behavior gate and the evidence required to evaluate it. Empty evidence does
not pass a gate.
