# Nangong Wan Calibration Case

Read this only to transfer process depth, artifact quality, desktop readability,
and stopping discipline. It is not a source of another target’s identity,
proportion, costume, action, or scheduler values.

## What transfers

Use this case to insist on evidence before selection, canonical identity at
actual size, role-based action coverage, representative risk pilots, semantic
key poses, separate visual/runtime/behavior gates, causal repair, long-use
observation, and a stop decision. The owned rules live in [Identity and
Evidence](identity-and-evidence.md), [Canonical Identity and
Proportions](canonical-identity-and-proportions.md), [Actions and
Motion](actions-and-motion.md), [Visual QA](visual-qa.md), [Repair and
Convergence](repair-and-convergence.md), and [Behavior and
Soak](behavior-and-soak.md).

When a project explicitly selects a Nangong Wan package, canonical identity,
contact sheet, timed preview, review report, or test record, record its path and
hash. Do not search neighboring experiments or assume the newest timestamp is
approved. The evidence base ends before later mouse-gaze/64-angle work,
rooftop-and-moon states, showcase videos, and later release experiments.

This case calibrates process depth, artifact quality, desktop readability,
and stopping discipline. It does not govern another pet's geometry,
costume volume, pixel width, action count, timing, distance, weight, or
cooldown without independent target-specific evidence.

## Record the transfer statement

For each new target, state both:

1. the copied process: evidence order, gates, artifact chain, repair logic, and
   stop discipline; and
2. the newly derived target-specific identity, actions, effects, frame counts,
   timing, movement, weights, and cooldowns.

Any identity, action, effect, frame, timing, movement, weight, or cooldown
value copied from the historical record instead of independently derived for the
current target is invalid, even when the process list is followed.
